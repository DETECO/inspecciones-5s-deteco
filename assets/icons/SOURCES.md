# Icon sources

All SVG icons except `award.svg` are unmodified Tabler outline icons, under the adjacent MIT license (`LICENSE-tabler-icons.txt`).

`award.svg` is the unmodified Google Material Icons outlined `workspace_premium` icon. It supplies the star and ribbons in the approved excellent-score reference. Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/src/social/workspace_premium/materialiconsoutlined/24px.svg . Licensed under Apache 2.0, included in `LICENSE-material-icons.txt`.
